So the water currently rising has to have a value, right? We have the stat in the home screen "Drink 50ml to clear". What if it's portrayed as "Water to drink: 50ml", displayed in the mini-overlay?

Yes! That connects the entire experience together.

Think about the user journey:

The water rises on their screen while they scroll Twitter.
They tap the squid handle to open the quick-log sheet.
They see the buttons: [150]  [250]  [330]  [500].
If the sheet tells them how much they need to clear the screen, their eye immediately knows which button to tap!

Phrasing: Actionable vs. Label
Instead of the robotic label "Water to drink: 250ml", use natural, active language:

"Drink ~250 ml to clear your screen" (or: "Drink ~250 ml to clear the water • 1.5 L of 2.5 L today")

Why this is brilliant UX:
Instant Guidance: If the sheet says "Drink ~250 ml to clear your screen", the user doesn't even have to think—they tap the [ 250 ] button, the water drains away, and they're back to what they were doing in 1 second.
Pairs with the Phantom-Debt Fix: When you cap the deficit at 500 ml, the number to clear the screen will always be between 50 ml and 500 ml, which matches your four quick-log buttons (150, 250, 330, 500) perfectly!
